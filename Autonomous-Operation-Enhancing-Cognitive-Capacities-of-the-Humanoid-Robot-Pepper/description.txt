Phase 1 focuses on enhancing Pepper's cognitive capabilities, enabling the robot to perceive, understand, and interpret 

its surrounding environment while learning to interact naturally with both humans and objects. During this phase, the emphasis 

is placed on developing high-level perception and interaction functionalities, including face recognition, object recognition, 

object grasping, and human–robot dialogue. To achieve these objectives, the system integrates Large Language Models (LLMs), 

Knowledge Graphs, Deep Learning, and Control Theory, providing the necessary foundations for intelligent perception, reasoning, 

decision-making, and interaction before addressing autonomous navigation in the subsequent phase.
